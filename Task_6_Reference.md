## How Task 7 Uses This Evidence

Task 7 uses the recordings and detection result to examine:

- Whether synthetic delivery can imply unsupported authority.
- Why accurate content still requires appropriate permission.
- How excerpting can alter attribution and remove disclosure.
- Why production volume must remain within review capacity.
- Why detection cannot replace factual and ethical review.

The four scenarios in Phase A are hypothetical. They are not claims
that these harms occurred during Task 6.
